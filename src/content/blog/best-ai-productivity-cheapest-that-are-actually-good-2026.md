There's a persistent myth in the software world: you get what you pay for, and anything cheap is a compromise. In the AI productivity space, that assumption is increasingly wrong. Several tools in 2026 offer free plans and sub-£20/month tiers that genuinely rival far pricier alternatives. The trick is knowing which ones are worth your time — and which are cheap for a reason.

Let's cut through the noise. Here are the best low-cost AI productivity tools across [presentations and meeting intelligence](/categories/ai-productivity) that don't make you feel like you've settled.

## The Free Plan Myth: Some Are Actually Generous

Most free plans are bait — hobbled until you pay. A few genuinely aren't.

**[tl;dv](/tools/tldv)** has perhaps the most remarkable free tier in the meeting recorder space: unlimited recordings and transcripts, AI notes, and support for Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams across 30+ languages. There's no minutes cap. For individuals or small teams who simply need reliable, searchable meeting records without paying anything, it's difficult to argue against it. The catch is that deeper features like multi-meeting AI insights and CRM integrations sit behind the Business plan (£59/month), so power users will eventually need to pay. But for getting started? It's unusually generous.

**[Fireflies.ai](/tools/fireflies-ai)** also offers a free plan with unlimited transcription and AI summaries — though storage is capped at 400 minutes total (not per month, total), and AI credits are limited to 20. It's workable for occasional use but can feel tight if you're in back-to-back calls all week.

**[Gamma](/tools/gamma)** gives you a free plan with unlimited deck creation and AI presentation generation. The 400 credits are one-time only, not monthly, which is worth understanding before you dive in. Once they're spent, you're looking at the Plus plan at $9/month — one of the better-value paid tiers in the presentation category.

## Best Cheap Paid Plans: Where £10–£20/Month Actually Gets You Somewhere

If you're willing to spend a little, the value available at the lower paid tiers is genuinely impressive.

**[MeetGeek](/tools/meetgeek)** at $9.99/month (Pro) gives you 20 hours of transcription per month, a full year of storage, integrations with Slack, Notion, and CRMs, plus AI Chat for querying past meetings. It's also headquartered in Bucharest and built with GDPR compliance in mind — a meaningful differentiator for European teams who care about data residency. The free plan only offers 3 hours a month, so if you have regular calls, the Pro tier is where MeetGeek becomes genuinely useful.

**[Sembly AI](/tools/sembly-ai)** at $10/month (Professional) offers unlimited meetings, AI-generated tasks and action items, custom vocabulary, and integrations with Slack and CRMs. The agentic task automation angle sets it apart from simpler transcribers — it doesn't just note what was said, it starts doing something about it. For project managers drowning in follow-ups, this is worth a look.

**[Gamma](/tools/gamma)** at $9/month (Plus) removes branding, gives you 1,000 AI credits per month, and unlocks advanced image models and custom fonts. For non-designers who need polished presentations regularly — marketers, educators, early-stage founders — this is one of the best value-for-money tools in the category. It's not PowerPoint, and design control is deliberately limited, but that's the point: it makes decisions for you, quickly.

## When Cheap Isn't Right: Know When to Spend More

Not every situation suits a budget tool. Here's where the low-cost options fall short:

- **Conversation intelligence at scale**: If your sales team needs advanced coaching, playbooks, and multi-meeting pattern analysis, tl;dv's Business plan or Fireflies' Business tier are more appropriate. The free and entry-level plans don't get you there.
- **Multilingual transcription-first workflows**: **[Notta](/tools/notta)** supports 104 languages and is purpose-built for transcription, but the free plan's 3-minute-per-conversation cap makes it impractical for real meetings. The Pro plan at $13.99/month is solid for journalists, researchers, and students — but if meeting intelligence is the goal, Fireflies or Sembly offer more.
- **Brand-consistent presentations across a team**: **[Beautiful.ai](/tools/beautiful-ai)** starts at $12/month and has no free plan, but its brand guardrails and locked slide features make it the right choice for sales teams that need consistency. Gamma is better for solo use; Beautiful.ai is better when a team needs to stay on-brand.

## The Bottom Line

Cheap AI productivity tools have grown up. In 2026, you don't need to spend a fortune to get reliable meeting transcription, smart AI notes, or polished presentations. For most individuals and small teams, **tl;dv's free plan** or **MeetGeek Pro at $9.99/month** cover meeting intelligence well. For presentations, **Gamma's free or Plus plan** is hard to beat at the price point.

The key is matching the tool to your actual workflow — not just grabbing the cheapest option available. Overpaying for features you'll never touch is as wasteful as underpaying for something that breaks under pressure.
