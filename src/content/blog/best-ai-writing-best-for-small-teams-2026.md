Picture this: your four-person marketing team is juggling a product launch, three blog posts, and a week's worth of social content — all due Friday. Someone suggests trialling an AI writing tool. You sign up for the first one that appears in Google, only to discover the collaboration features are locked behind an enterprise tier that costs more than your entire software budget. Sound familiar?

Small teams have specific needs that most AI writing tool comparisons ignore. You need **shared access**, **brand consistency**, and a price that makes sense when split across a handful of seats — not a solo-user licence stretched uncomfortably across a Slack channel. Here's what actually works in 2026.

## The Collaboration-First Pick: Copy.ai

If genuine team functionality is your priority, [Copy.ai](/tools/copy-ai) is the standout choice at this end of the market. The Starter plan gives you **five user seats, unlimited words, and brand voice** — all features you'd typically expect to pay enterprise prices for. Check official pricing for the current rate, but it works out to a sensible per-person figure for a small team.

The 90+ writing tools cover the usual suspects — social captions, email sequences, ad copy, blog outlines — and the Chat by Copy.ai interface makes it approachable for teammates who aren't natural writers. The Infobase feature lets you store company information centrally, so everyone is writing from the same playbook.

The caveats are real: there are no SEO-specific features built in, and workflow automation is reserved for higher tiers. If your team's output is heavy on long-form SEO content, Copy.ai alone may feel limiting.

## The SEO-Focused Alternative: Writesonic

For teams where search visibility matters, [Writesonic](/tools/writesonic) brings a different set of strengths. The Individual plan at $39/mo includes Chatsonic, an Article Writer (check official features for the current version), brand voice, and built-in SEO tools — features that would require third-party integrations with most rivals.

The Standard plan at $99/mo unlocks more words and priority support, which may be worth it if your team is producing content at volume. Bear in mind that word limits on lower tiers can become a friction point quickly, and the interface has a lot going on — expect a short onboarding period before the team finds their rhythm.

Writesonic doesn't have dedicated multi-seat pricing in the same way Copy.ai does, so it works best when one person owns the account and acts as the content lead, rather than a fully distributed team workflow.

## The Budget Safety Net: HIX.AI

[HIX.AI](/tools/hix-ai) deserves a mention for teams watching every pound. The Basic plan at $19.99/mo gives you access to 120+ tools, the HIX Editor for long-form work, and an email writer — all running on unlimited GPT-3.5 words. If your team's needs are relatively straightforward and GPT-4 access isn't essential day-to-day, this is a remarkably capable toolkit for the price.

Upgrading to Pro ($49.99/mo) unlocks GPT-4, the full long-form editor, and priority support. It's worth noting that HIX.AI is a newer brand with less of a track record than some rivals, and the lack of dedicated team seats means you'll need to decide how you manage shared access practically.

## What About Jasper and Rytr?

[Jasper](/tools/jasper) is excellent — brand voice training, campaign management, SEO mode, and 50+ templates are all genuinely useful. But at $59/mo minimum with no free plan, it's built for marketing teams at scale or agencies, not a scrappy four-person outfit trying to keep costs lean. File it away for when the team grows.

[Rytr](/tools/rytr), meanwhile, is the opposite extreme. At $7.50/mo for unlimited characters, it's almost implausibly cheap — and it shows its value for solo writers or non-English content (35+ languages on the Premium plan). The problem for small teams is that there are no team features on basic plans and limited brand voice training. It's a strong personal tool, not a shared workspace.

## The Honest Takeaway

For most small teams in 2026, **Copy.ai's Starter plan is the pragmatic choice** — it's built with collaboration in mind, covers a broad range of content tasks, and the per-seat cost is genuinely reasonable. If SEO content is central to your strategy, layer in Writesonic for that specific workflow. And if budget is very tight, HIX.AI's Basic plan punches well above its price tag.

Before you commit to anything, make sure your whole team can trial it properly. Most tools here offer a free plan or trial — use it hard for a week with real briefs, not demo content.
