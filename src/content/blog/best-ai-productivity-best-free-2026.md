Finding a truly useful free AI tool is harder than it sounds. Most "free" plans are little more than glorified trials — a handful of credits, a tight time limit, and a wall that appears the moment you try to do anything meaningful. The tools below are different: each has a free tier worth actually using. But every free plan has a catch. Here's what you genuinely get, what you give up, and who each tool is right for.

## Free AI Presentation Tools

### Gamma — Best free option for one-off decks

[Gamma](/tools/gamma) lets you generate a polished presentation, document, or webpage from a prompt in seconds. The free plan gives you **400 one-time AI credits** and unlimited decks — the key word being *one-time*. Once those credits are spent, you cannot top them up without upgrading. For someone who needs to produce a handful of investor decks, training materials, or landing pages and doesn't mind the Gamma watermark, that's a genuinely useful runway.

What you give up: Gamma branding on all published work, no custom fonts, and access only to standard AI image models. Design control is also lighter than PowerPoint, so if you need pixel-perfect layouts, look elsewhere.

**Verdict:** Right for non-designers who need occasional, polished output fast. Wrong for anyone with ongoing, high-volume deck production — the credits will run dry.

### Beautiful.ai — No free plan; skip it if budget is tight

[Beautiful.ai](/tools/beautiful-ai) is excellent at enforcing brand consistency across sales teams, but there is **no free plan** — only a 14-day trial. At $12/mo (Pro) or $40/mo (Team), it's a considered purchase. If you're evaluating free options specifically, it shouldn't be on your shortlist.

## Free AI Meeting Note-Takers

This is where the free-plan landscape gets genuinely competitive. Four strong contenders exist, and choosing between them comes down to a few key variables: how many meetings you have per month, whether you need CRM integrations, and how important multilingual support is.

### tl;dv — A very generous free plan for recordings and notes

[tl;dv](/tools/tldv) offers a notably capable free tier: recordings, transcripts, and AI notes across Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams in 30+ languages. Check the [official tl;dv pricing page](https://tldv.io) to confirm the current limits on recordings and storage, as these details can change — but the free plan has historically been among the most generous in this category.

The trade-offs are real, though. CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot) are paywalled behind the Pro tier, and the genuinely powerful multi-meeting AI insights — where tl;dv synthesises patterns across many calls — require the Business plan. It also uses a meeting bot, which some participants find intrusive. But if you simply want reliable transcription and AI notes at no cost, tl;dv is a strong contender.

**Verdict:** Best free meeting recorder for individuals and small teams who don't yet need CRM sync.

### Fireflies.ai — Good free tier, but storage is a hard limit

[Fireflies.ai](/tools/fireflies-ai) includes AI summaries and action item detection on its free plan — genuinely useful features. The catch is **400 minutes of total storage**. That's not per month; that's ever. A busy professional can hit that ceiling in a fortnight. Once you do, you're upgrading or losing history. Note that unlimited transcription is not a feature of the free plan — storage constraints apply from the start.

It supports 100+ languages and has strong conversation intelligence features on paid tiers, making it a solid choice for sales teams willing to pay.

**Verdict:** Try it free, but budget for Pro ($10/mo) if you meet frequently.

### MeetGeek — Best free option for European teams

[MeetGeek](/tools/meetgeek) is headquartered in Bucharest and explicitly positions itself as GDPR-friendly — a meaningful differentiator for European businesses wary of US data residency. The free Basic plan gives **3 hours of transcription per month** across 100+ languages, unlimited summaries, and a 3-month storage window.

Three hours per month is modest — roughly six 30-minute calls — but for consultants or small teams with lighter meeting loads, it's workable. Storage expiring after 3 months on the free plan is the biggest gotcha.

**Verdict:** The right call for European teams or consultants with fewer than 6–8 meetings per month.

### Sembly AI — Free tier is thin; better on paid plans

[Sembly AI](/tools/sembly-ai) has agentic workflow automation as its differentiator — it can detect action items and push them into downstream tools automatically. That's genuinely powerful. However, the free Personal plan has a **limited monthly minutes allowance** — Sembly does not publish the exact cap publicly, so check the [Sembly AI pricing page](https://www.sembly.ai) for the current figure before committing. Deeper analytics are reserved for paid tiers. It supports 40+ languages and covers Zoom, Meet, and Teams.

**Verdict:** Worth exploring if agentic task automation is your priority, but the free plan is the weakest of this group — verify the minute limit directly with Sembly before relying on it.

### Notta — Best free option for transcription-first users

[Notta](/tools/notta) supports a wide range of languages — check the [Notta pricing page](https://www.notta.ai) for the current count, as this can change — and is built around transcription quality first, meeting intelligence second. The free plan allows 120 minutes per month but caps each individual conversation at 3 minutes, which makes it nearly useless for live meetings. It's much better suited to journalists, researchers, or students uploading short audio clips for transcription.

**Verdict:** Excellent free tool for transcription of short recordings. Not the right fit for meeting notes.

## The Decision Rule

- **Need free presentations?** → [Gamma](/tools/gamma) — use those 400 credits wisely.
- **Need a generous free meeting recorder?** → [tl;dv](/tools/tldv) — check current plan limits, but it's consistently strong at zero cost.
- **In Europe or privacy-conscious?** → [MeetGeek](/tools/meetgeek) — GDPR-friendly and genuinely usable on the free tier.
- **Transcribing audio files in many languages?** → [Notta](/tools/notta) — wide language support and strong accuracy on short recordings.
- **Growing team ready to pay a little?** → [Fireflies.ai](/tools/fireflies-ai) at Pro ($10/mo) is excellent value once you outgrow the storage cap.

Free plans are a starting point, not a destination. The tools above have been chosen because their free tiers are genuinely useful — not just enough to frustrate you into upgrading. Use them to validate the workflow; upgrade only when the limits start costing you more time than money. If you're still uncertain which fits your situation best, don't guess.
